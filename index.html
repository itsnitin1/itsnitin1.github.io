About

Not the usual path into software.

I didn't start out wanting to write code. I started out on a manufacturing line at Salcomp India, inspecting electronic assemblies and making sure nothing left the floor with a defect. It was detail-heavy, repetitive in the best way, and it taught me something I still lean on: check your work against the spec, every single time, no matter how routine it feels.

From there I moved into a corporate role at STEAG Energy Services, supporting the Training & Advisory team — documentation, presentations, workshop coordination, keeping cross-functional projects on track. It's a different kind of work, but the same instinct applies: things need to be accurate, and someone has to own that.

Right now I'm doing both jobs at once, in a sense — working full-time and studying for a BCA at IGNOU in the evenings, while teaching myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Git, and how APIs actually work. I'm not going to call myself a developer yet. I'm someone who shows up consistently and is building toward it, the same way I built toward everything else.
